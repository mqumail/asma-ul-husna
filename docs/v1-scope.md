# v1 Scope

## Goal of v1
A user can learn the 99 names in order, one per day, from the app and a home
screen widget, on Android and iOS, without an account or internet.

## In v1 (must have)
- Onboarding: welcome, explain one name a day, set a reminder time (optional)
- Today's name card: Arabic with tashkeel, transliteration, short meaning,
  longer meaning, a dua in English
- Daily unlock at local Sunrise
- Personal journey: everyone starts at name 1 on install
- Past names can be revisited; future names stay locked
- Missed days: names advance by calendar
- Progress grid of all 99 (unlocked / today / locked)
- Daily reminder notification at a chosen time
- Home screen widget on Android and iOS (small and medium)
- Placeholder art: gradient + geometric pattern per name
- Restarting after day 99
- Light and dark mode
- About screen: sources, content reviewer, credits
- Works fully offline; no account, no ads, no tracking
- English only

## Nice to have (only if everything above is done)
- Favourites
- Share today's name as an image
- iOS lock screen widget, large widget size
- Android auto-wallpaper; iOS "save wallpaper to Photos"

## Not in v1 (later versions)
- Real illustrations for all 99
- Audio pronunciation
- Fajr or Maghrib unlock option
- Other languages (Urdu, Malay, Turkish…) and right-to-left UI
- Review or quiz mode, and anything after day 99 beyond starting again
- iOS Shortcuts wallpaper automation
- Accounts, sync, community features
- Payments or donations (suggested: free at launch)

## What happens after day 99 in v1
Show a completion screen and offer to start round two from name 1.

## Definition of done for v1
- Live on Google Play and the App Store
- All 99 names reviewed by a scholar
- Unlock logic covered by tests; CI green
- Tested on at least 2 Android phones and 1 iPhone
